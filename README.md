An entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue"](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

`main.py` writes one original 100-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt`:
a dark lord who conquered the Ashen Coast, holds the hero's sword,
prophecy and fellowship chained in the east wing, and waits for Friday's
eclipse — while the paperwork, the ravens that left a note, and the
calendar keep getting in the way. His tiredness lives in the rhythm,
the pauses and the small deflections, never in a plain statement that
he is weary. The last line is the sting.

Run it with `python3 main.py` (stdlib only, no arguments, no network).
